# Awesome GDPR [![Awesome](https://awesome.re/badge-flat.svg)](https://awesome.re)

[<img src="GDPR.png" align="right" width="300">](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679)

The General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) is a regulation on data protection and privacy for all individuals within the European Union and the European Economic Area. The regulation has increased the focus on privacy in companies and strengthened the data subjects influence.

## GDPR Case Law - Court of Justice of the European Union (CJEU)

### Case Law Databases and Resources
* [GDPRhub](https://gdprhub.eu/index.php?title=Welcome_to_GDPRhub) - Free and open wiki with GDPR case law from across Europe, including CJEU decisions.
* [DPcuria.eu](https://www.dpcuria.eu/) - Specialized database of CJEU data protection case law.
* [CJEU Data Protection Fact Sheet (2025)](https://curia.europa.eu/) - Updated fact sheet summarizing key GDPR case law (covering judgments before July 2024).
* [activeMind.legal - GDPR Judgments](https://www.activemind.legal/rulings/) - Judgments of the ECJ and Member State courts on GDPR.
* [Digibeetle - Pending CJEU Cases](https://app.digibeetle.eu/pending-cases/) - Tracker for pending and decided CJEU data protection cases.
* [EDPS Court Cases](https://www.edps.europa.eu/data-protection/data-protection/case-law-and-guidance_en) - Case law and guidance.

### Major CJEU Decisions (2024-2025)

#### 2025 Judgments
* **[Case C-203/22 (February 27, 2025)](https://curia.europa.eu/)** - Algorithmic transparency vs. trade secrets - Balance between automated decision-making transparency rights and secret protection in credit scoring.
* **[Case C-710/23 (April 3, 2025)](https://curia.europa.eu/)** - Ministerstvo zdravotnictví - Data concerning representatives of legal persons.
* **[Case C-655/23 (April 30, 2025)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/HTML/?uri=CELEX:62023CJ0655)** - Legal remedies under Chapter VIII GDPR - Remedies available under Articles 77-79.
* **[Case C-413/23 P (September 4, 2025)](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/PDF/?uri=CELEX:62023CJ0413)** - Personal data definitions, identifiability, pseudonymised data, and consent requirements.

#### 2024 Judgments
* **[Case C-21/23 - Lindenapotheke (October 4, 2024)](https://curia.europa.eu/)** - Non-exhaustive harmonization of GDPR remedies.
* **[Case C-507/23 (October 4, 2024)](https://curia.europa.eu/)** - Data protection principles.
* **[Case C-65/23 (December 19, 2024)](https://curia.europa.eu/)** - K GmbH - Processing of employees' personal data.
* **[Case C-757/22 (July 11, 2024)](https://curia.europa.eu/)** - Meta Platforms Ireland - Representative action mechanisms.
* **Multiple decisions (October 4, 2024)** - Five important GDPR compensation and remedies decisions.

#### Key GDPR Topics Addressed by CJEU
* Automated decision-making and transparency (Art. 22)
* GDPR fine calculations for subsidiaries
* Public bodies as data controllers
* Pseudonymised data and identifiability
* Data subject rights and effective remedies
* International data transfers
* Employee data processing
* Representative actions and collective redress

### Landmark Pre-2024 GDPR Cases (Reference)
* **[Schrems II - Case C-311/18 (2020)](https://curia.europa.eu/)** - Invalidation of Privacy Shield, requirements for international transfers.
* **[Case C-77/21 - Digi](https://gdprhub.eu/index.php?title=CJEU_-_C-77/21_-_Digi)** - Important GDPR principles case.
